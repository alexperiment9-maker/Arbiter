# The Arbiter

Disputes of the self, weighed and settled.

A decision maker app: a loaded coin, ten thousand simulated futures, and an old voice behind a candle.
Runs fully offline. All cases are saved on your own device only.

## Put it on GitHub (no coding)

1. Go to github.com and sign in. Click **+** (top right) → **New repository**.
2. Name it `arbiter`. Set it to **Public**. Click **Create repository**.
3. On the new page, click **uploading an existing file**.
4. Drag in all 6 files from this folder:
   `index.html`, `manifest.webmanifest`, `sw.js`, `icon-192.png`, `icon-512.png`, `README.md`
5. Click **Commit changes**.
6. Go to **Settings** → **Pages** (left menu).
7. Under **Branch**, choose `main` and `/ (root)` → **Save**.
8. Wait 1–2 minutes. Your app will be at:
   `https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/arbiter/`

## Install on your phone

1. Open that link in Chrome.
2. Tap **⋮** → **Add to Home screen** (or **Install app**).
3. Open it from the home screen. It now works with no internet.

## Updating later

Upload the new `index.html` over the old one (same steps 3–5).
If the phone still shows the old version, close the app fully and open it twice.
